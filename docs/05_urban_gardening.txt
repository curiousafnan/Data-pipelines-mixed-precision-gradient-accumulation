COMMUNITY GARDEN SEASONAL OPERATIONS PLAN
Date: 21 March 2025
Site: Westbrook Neighborhood Garden

The Westbrook community garden is preparing twenty-four raised beds for the spring planting season. Members will add finished compost, inspect irrigation tubing, and test soil moisture before distributing seedlings. A volunteer committee will allocate beds according to household availability and accessibility needs.

The seasonal plan emphasizes low-water gardening practices. Mulch will be applied after transplanting, rain barrels will be inspected for leaks, and the irrigation schedule will be adjusted during hot weather. Gardeners will record the dates of planting, flowering, and harvest so that future seasons can be planned using local observations.

Weekly workdays will focus on path maintenance, pest inspection, and shared-tool cleaning. Produce grown in the communal beds will be distributed through the neighborhood food pantry. At the end of the season, volunteers will review yields and prepare a list of improvements for the following year.
