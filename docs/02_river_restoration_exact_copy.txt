RIVER RESTORATION FIELD REPORT
Date: 12 September 2025
Location: North Valley Watershed

The North Valley restoration team assessed a two-kilometre section of river following a season of unusually heavy rainfall. Volunteers recorded erosion along the eastern bank, measured water clarity at five sampling points, and photographed native vegetation returning to the floodplain.

The team installed biodegradable erosion-control matting, removed discarded plastic from the riverbank, and planted willow and alder saplings in areas where root systems could stabilize the soil. Water samples will be collected every two weeks to monitor suspended sediment and temperature. The restoration coordinator recommended keeping foot traffic away from newly planted sections until the end of winter.

During the next visit, staff will compare photographs taken from fixed observation points and count surviving saplings. The project aims to reduce bank erosion while restoring habitat for aquatic insects and small fish.
